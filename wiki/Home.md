# Spek — Wiki

**Structured knowledge. Reusable forms. Multilingual publications.**

Spek is an experimental, local-first semantic publishing engine. It binds **typed semantic units** and short multilingual expressions into a **library of reusable editorial layouts**, then emits static HTML, PDF, and XeLaTeX.

**The idea:** *Crystallize the form; vary the content.* A semantic claim can be presented as a scannable result card, an argument map, or a comparison without asking the language system to generate long continuous paragraphs.

## Trust and qualification

The demo is **not an empirical scientific result**. Its phrases are written test fixtures, not certified GF/PGF SemantiK RuntimeSet outputs. The compiler neither authenticates Kristal canonical commitments nor grants the right to publish. Read [[Epistemic and Security Boundaries]] before considering a real release.
